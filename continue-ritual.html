BEFORE DAY ONE

How to Prepare Your Bowl

The same four steps, every morning. It gets faster — and quieter — each day.

01

Sift. One level scoop (chashaku) of matcha through the furui, into the chawan. This breaks up clumps — the step most people skip, and the reason store-bought matcha tastes gritty.

02

Add water. About 70ml of water just off the boil (roughly 80°C — let boiled water sit 1 minute). Too hot and the matcha turns bitter.

03

Whisk. Hold the chasen loosely, whisk in a brisk "W" motion for 15–20 seconds until a fine foam forms on top.

04

Sit. No phone. Drink slowly. This is the part most people rush — don't. The guide book has a one-line intention for each day.

YOUR SEVEN DAYS

What to Expect, Morning by Morning

01MON

Start with the everyday matcha.

Use the first tin. Don't aim for a perfect bowl — aim for a finished one. Read the day-one intention card before you whisk, not after.

02TUE

Notice the energy curve.

Pay attention to how you feel at hour two and hour four, compared to a normal coffee morning. Most people notice the difference starting today.

03WED

This is the day it usually breaks — or sticks.

If you're tempted to skip, do the shortest version: sift, whisk, drink, no sitting. Two minutes. The habit survives on completion, not intensity.

04THU

Try the guided audio meditation.

Optional, 10–20 minutes, after your bowl. It's in the guide book's QR code on page 3. No app required — it plays straight from your browser.

05FRI

Switch to the second matcha.

A different profile — slower, rounder. Notice what changes in taste and in how you feel by mid-morning.

06SAT

Take your time.

No morning rush today. Extend the "sit" step — five extra minutes with the empty bowl in your hands before you move on.

07SUN

Your last bowl from this kit.

Use whatever matcha remains. This is a good morning to decide: keep going, or let it go. Either is fine — but if you want to keep going, read below.
