WHY THIS EXISTS

You Want a Morning Practice.
But You Don't Know Where to Start.

◯

"I start things and stop them after a week."

Most rituals fail not because of willpower — but because the effort of beginning is too high each day. When everything arrives ready, the only thing left is to show up.

◯

"I want to add mindfulness to my mornings but meditation apps don't work for me."

Screens in the morning are counterproductive. A physical ritual — hands, water, a bowl — grounds you in a way that an app cannot. It doesn't require you to focus. It gives you something to focus on.

◯

"I drink coffee every day and I'm tired of how it makes me feel."

Matcha provides a sustained, clean energy through the combination of caffeine and L-theanine — without the spike or crash. The ritual of making it sets the tone for the day before you've opened a single tab.

The 7-Day Ritual doesn't ask you to overhaul your routine. It asks for seven minutes, once a day, for seven days. At the end of the week, most people order the next one without thinking about it. That is the point.
