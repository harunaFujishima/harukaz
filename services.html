TASTING EXPERIENCE

At-Home Tasting Experience

CAD $60 / session

At your home or chosen location  ·  60 minutes  ·  Toronto and GTA

Before matcha lattes, matcha was a tea prepared in the study of Chado (茶道 — the Way of Tea) in Japan: a spiritual and philosophical practice rooted in ritual, where the principles applied in the tea space extend to daily life. In this 60-minute session, HARUKAZ demonstrates the art of making matcha while sharing its history, philosophy, and the connection between ourselves, the tools, the tea, and the present moment.

The Experience

  • Act I — Context: The history and philosophy of Chado. Why matcha is prepared this way, and what it asks of the one preparing it.
  • Act II — Demonstration: A full preparation of your bowl, narrated in real time. You observe before you participate.
  • Act III — Hands-On: You prepare your own bowl using the same wares. Guidance provided at every step.
  • Tasting & Reflection: A silent tasting with guided sensory reflection. What do you notice?
  • All equipment and ceramics brought by HARUKAZ

PRIVATE CEREMONY

Private Matcha Experience

CAD $60 / session

Individuals, couples, or small groups  ·  60 minutes  ·  Your location

An intimate ceremony shaped entirely around you. We begin with a grounding practice — a deliberate pause before anything is prepared. Then the bowl is made in silence, with your full attention invited at every step. You are guided to notice texture, temperature, colour, and the quality of your own mind before and after the bowl. No performance. No audience. Just presence.

The Experience

  • Opening: A 5-minute grounding practice. We arrive and settle before anything begins.
  • Preparation: The bowl is made with care — measuring, sifting, whisking — each step an act of attention.
  • The Tasting: You receive your bowl in silence. Sip slowly. Notice what shifts.
  • Reflection: A brief guided reflection on what the ritual revealed.
  • Ceremonial-grade Uji matcha, chawan, and chasen provided
  • Small take-home matcha portion + printed tasting card
